Primary sources and documented trends

Short, sourced material on Islamic texts, demographic data, and recorded outcomes. Interactive questions below test specific claims against the cited sources.

Sample questions

True or false: The Quran contains verses that prescribe fighting those who do not believe until they pay the jizya or are subdued.

Quran 9:29 (Sahih International): “Fight those who do not believe in Allah or in the Last Day and who do not consider unlawful what Allah and His Messenger have made unlawful and who do not adopt the religion of truth from those who were given the Scripture — [fight] until they give the jizyah willingly while they are humbled.”

True or false: Pew Research surveys in multiple Muslim-majority countries have found substantial percentages favoring sharia as official law.

Pew Research Center, “The World’s Muslims: Religion, Politics and Society” (2013). In several countries majorities or large minorities said they favor making sharia the official law of the land. Exact figures vary by country; see the full report for tables.
