Selected Authors and Researchers

Individuals with published books or peer-reviewed work who have examined Islamic doctrine, historical expansion, or contemporary integration outcomes.

Ayaan Hirsi Ali

Somali-born author and former Dutch parliamentarian. Books include Infidel and Nomad. Writes on apostasy, women’s status under classical Islamic law, and integration difficulties in Europe.

Bernard Lewis

Historian of the Middle East (Princeton). Works such as What Went Wrong? and The Crisis of Islam examine the historical encounter between Islamic societies and the modern West.

Ibn Warraq

Pseudonymous author of Why I Am Not a Muslim and editor of collections on Quranic criticism. Focuses on textual and historical analysis.
