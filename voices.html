Selected Authors and Researchers

Individuals who have examined Islamic doctrine, historical expansion, or contemporary outcomes using primary sources or direct experience.

Harris Sultan

Australian author and YouTuber (ex-Muslim, born in Pakistan). Book: The Curse of God: Why I Left Islam. Channel: Harris Sultan on YouTube. Discusses classical texts, apostasy, and demographic trends without sensationalism.

Sam Harris

Neuroscientist and author. Public talks and writings examine the doctrinal basis for intolerance, jihad, and apostasy penalties in Islam. See his site and podcast for primary-source discussions.

Richard Dawkins

Evolutionary biologist. Has stated that Islamic doctrines contain active hostility toward women and homosexuals and that critics of those doctrines are often mislabeled. Public lectures and writings available online.

Ayaan Hirsi Ali

Somali-born author. Books include Infidel and Nomad. Writes on apostasy, women’s status under classical law, and European integration data.

Documented incidents

Charlie Hebdo and related attacks (2015)

Gunmen killed 12 at the Paris offices of Charlie Hebdo over cartoons of Muhammad. Subsequent attacks in Paris (Bataclan) killed 130. Official investigations attributed them to Islamist networks citing religious motives.

Salman Rushdie

Author of The Satanic Verses has lived under fatwa-related threat since 1989. Stabbed in 2022. The original fatwa and ongoing threats are matters of public record.

UK and European knife and vehicle attacks

Multiple incidents (Manchester Arena 2017, London Bridge, etc.) were investigated as Islamist terrorism. National crime and counter-terrorism reports list the perpetrators’ stated motives and prior radicalization indicators.
