Quick Facts

Apostasy

Classical Islamic jurisprudence and several modern penal codes in Muslim-majority countries treat leaving Islam as a capital offense or prescribe severe penalties. See References for specific national laws and fiqh sources.

Parallel societies

Official reports from Sweden, France, and the United Kingdom have documented concentrated areas with elevated rates of certain violent crimes, lower employment, and reduced cooperation with authorities. See national statistical agencies and government inquiries listed in References.

Survey attitudes

Pew Research (2013) and subsequent national polls show large minorities or majorities in multiple Muslim populations favoring sharia punishments for theft, adultery, or apostasy, and opposing equal inheritance or testimony rights for women. Figures differ by country and generation.
